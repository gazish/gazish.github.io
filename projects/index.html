  • MSc thesisStockholm University, with Vladimir Blaskov

    Multi-Criteria Threat Evaluation for Local Climate Hazards

    A reusable MCDA template that helps municipalities prioritise local climate hazards: weighted criteria, rubric scoring, survey-elicited stakeholder priorities, uncertainty ratings and best- and worst-case rank intervals.

    Demonstrated on municipality cases in Tampere, Tartu and Malmö. Published on DiVA.

    • MCDA
    • Survey elicitation
    • Uncertainty
    • Sensitivity analysis

A full project on real public data, with the code on GitHub.

  • Interactive dashboardUCI Online Retail II, real public data

    Online retail sales: SQL, Python and a KPI dashboard

    1.07 million invoice lines from a UK online retailer, cleaned in SQL with every raw row accounted for, defined KPIs, cohort retention, RFM segments and a revenue bridge, checked in pandas and shown in an interactive dashboard.

    Net sales were flat (+1.9%) and sales to identified customers fell 1.3%; growth came from international markets and from sales with no customer ID.

    • SQL
    • DuckDB
    • Python
    • pandas
    • KPI design
    • Cohorts
    • RFM
