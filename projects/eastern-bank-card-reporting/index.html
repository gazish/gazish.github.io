Work · Eastern Bank PLC · Jul 2023 to Aug 2024

Card portfolio analysis and monthly reporting

As Junior Officer in Cards Data and Reporting, I analysed transaction and revenue data for the bank's corporate and co-brand cards with SQL, and built monthly revenue and portfolio reporting for senior management.

Role
Junior Officer, Cards Data and Reporting
Where
Eastern Bank PLC, Dhaka
When
Jul 2023 to Aug 2024
Scale
About 25k card accounts, 150k+ transactions a month, 120+ branches
Tools
SQL, Excel (Power Query, PivotTables)
Context
Active co-brand users grew 12% over the period

The bank's data and reports are confidential, so this page describes the work without showing them.

Problem

The corporate and co-brand card portfolio had about 25,000 accounts and more than 150,000 transactions a month. My job was to turn that data into analysis of where and how the cards were used, and into monthly revenue and portfolio reporting for senior management.

Approach

  1. Query the transaction and revenue dataI wrote SQL against the transaction and revenue data to answer questions about usage and income across the card portfolio.
  2. Look at co-brand usage by regionI broke co-brand card usage down by region to find where it was concentrated, and flagged those regions for acquisition.
  3. Build the monthly reportingI built the monthly revenue and portfolio reports in Excel, using Power Query and PivotTables on top of SQL extracts, for senior management.
  4. Support the branchesI was the data and product point of contact for more than 120 branches.

Tools

  • SQL for the transaction and revenue analysis and for the extracts behind the reports.
  • Excel with Power Query to load and shape the extracts, and PivotTables for the monthly reports.

Result

Senior management had monthly revenue and portfolio reporting built on SQL extracts, and the regions where co-brand usage was concentrated were flagged for acquisition.

Active co-brand users grew 12% over the same period. Many things drive that number, so I give it as context, not as a result of my analysis alone.

What I would change

  • Connect Power Query to the database directly, so the monthly report refreshes without manual extracts.
  • Track activation and retention of new co-brand cardholders by region, to see whether acquisition in the flagged regions brings in active users.
