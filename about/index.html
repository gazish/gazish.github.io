I am a data analyst based in Stockholm. I work with SQL, Excel and Looker Studio, and I have an MSc in Data Science, Statistics and Decision Analysis from Stockholm University.

I studied Computer Science and Engineering at North South University and then worked in a few roles where reporting was part of the job: survey data quality and weekly stakeholder reports at FZ Technologies, and procurement and reporting at Tech Valley Networks.

My interest in data grew during my banking career. At Eastern Bank I worked in Cards Data and Reporting, where I analysed transaction and revenue data with SQL and built monthly reports for senior management. What I enjoyed most was summarising and visualising large datasets. That is what led me to the MSc in Stockholm.

The MSc added statistics and decision analysis. My thesis, written with Vladimir Blaskov, built a template that helps municipalities decide which local climate hazards to prioritise, using stakeholder priorities from a survey, uncertainty ratings and rank intervals. Alongside my studies I volunteered as a data analyst for Erasmus Student Network, where I replaced a manual monthly report with three Looker Studio dashboards.

I am now looking for a Data Analyst or BI Analyst role in Sweden or elsewhere in the EU, and I am available immediately. I am learning Swedish at SFI (course 3C).
